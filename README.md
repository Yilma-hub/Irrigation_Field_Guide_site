# Irrigation Field Guide

A personal, non-commercial field guide built on the open textbook

> Eisenhauer, D. E., Martin, D. L., Heeren, D. M., & Hoffman, G. J. (2021). *Irrigation Systems Management*. ASABE. https://doi.org/10.13031/ISM.2021

The book is licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Its text, figures and tables appear here unchanged and credited. The calculators, search, field notes and notes boxes were added for this guide and are not part of the book.

## What is inside

- All 15 chapters, section by section, with the book's own wording
- All 221 figures and 80 tables as printed, each with a page listing every passage that refers to it
- Every worked example, as printed
- A calculator for every formula in the book, plus table lookups and step-by-step procedures; every input and result works in US or metric units, and most calculators can solve for any input
- A unit converter and the book's conversion pages
- Search that groups answers: a quick answer, calculators, book passages, tables, figures, examples and your own notes
- A notes box under every section and calculator, saved on the device, with export and import
- A Workflow section with three flow diagrams (water management, system management and evaluation, design) connecting every calculator, plus a written explanation of how it all connects
- On every calculator: what it is used for in the field, grower questions with worked answers, the related book figures, and the calculators that feed it and that it feeds
- Study: over 2,000 multiple-choice questions from the book with spaced review, progress by chapter, figures and tables in the questions, and links back to the book and calculators
- Notes on every unit in the converter: what it means, where it is used, and how the conversion factor is derived
- Works offline after the first visit

## Put it on GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository at https://github.com/new, for example `irrigation-field-guide`. GitHub Pages on a free account needs it to be public.
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in everything from this folder (keep the `css`, `data`, `img` and `js` folders as folders), and commit.
3. In the repository go to Settings, then Pages. Under "Build and deployment" choose "Deploy from a branch", branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`, and save.
4. After a minute or two the guide is at `https://<your-user-name>.github.io/irrigation-field-guide/`.

## Install it as an app

Once the site is live on GitHub Pages:

- Android (Chrome): open the address, tap the menu (three dots), then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
- iPhone (Safari): open the address, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

It then opens full screen from its own icon and works without signal, because every page, table, figure and calculator is stored on the device after the first visit.

## Notes stay on your device

Notes are stored in the browser on each device. Use My notes, then Export notes, to keep a copy or move them to another phone.
